# Apple iPhone 17 Pro product-viewer model

- Source page: https://www.apple.com/iphone-17-pro/
- Apple scene: `iPhone17Pro_US_L`
- Retrieved: 2026-08-28
- Original scene manifest: https://www.apple.com/v/iphone-17-pro/h/static/scenes/iPhone17Pro_US_L_ktx.lsd

The GLTF geometry, binary data, UVs and KTX2 PBR textures in this directory
are the assets loaded by Apple's own product viewer. The portfolio keeps the
geometry, scale and orientation authored by Apple. The portfolio applies the
official `Orange` material-state values, plus an invisible screen hit-area and
the rotation interaction.
